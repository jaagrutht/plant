Pinwheel Flower Plant

Scientific name: Tabernaemontana divaricata

Origin: South and Southeast Asia

Uses: Ornamental and Landscaping Uses: The pinwheel flower is a popular choice for gardens and landscapes due to its beautiful white flowers and glossy green leaves. The pleasent fragrance of the flower adds to the ornamental value.
Medicinal Uses: It's used in ayurvedic medicine and eye remedies.

Description: It is a shrub with white, fragrant, and pinwheel-shaped flowers. It has glossy, dark green leaves and is known for its year-round flowering. The flowers have a distinctive fragrance, particularly noticeable in the evening.
It prefers partial shade to full sun and well-drained soil. It is relatively easy to grow and can be used as a specimen plant, hedge, or mass planting.

Some other names: Crape Jasmine, East Indian Rosebay, Nero's Crown, Nandi Vardhanam
